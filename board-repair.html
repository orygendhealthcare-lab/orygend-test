Board-level repair

Professional repair for medical equipment boards

We service medical equipment boards across brands and models, with component-level diagnosis and repair focused on restoring the equipment rather than replacing a good system because of a bad fault.

Where required, we import suitable ICs and specialized electronic components to preserve the quality, performance and longevity of the repair and the equipment.

Our board repair approach

Repair the fault. Preserve the equipment.

We work at board and component level to identify the actual failure point before recommending replacement. Our aim is to restore the state of the equipment and extend its useful service life.
