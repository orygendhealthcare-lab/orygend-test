About Orygend Healthcare

A dependable technical partner for the equipment hospitals rely on every day.

Orygend Healthcare provides biomedical equipment service with a practical focus on uptime, reliability and responsible repair. We support hospitals, clinics and diagnostic environments through preventive maintenance, AMC/CMC, breakdown support and board-level repair. From our main service unit in Bangalore, our technical team works to identify the real cause of failure, communicate the repair path clearly and return equipment to service with the attention it deserves.

Why we serve

Critical medical equipment should not become unusable simply because a fault appears in one module, board or component. We serve to help healthcare facilities reduce avoidable downtime, extend the useful life of equipment and make technically sound repair a practical alternative when complete replacement is not necessary.
