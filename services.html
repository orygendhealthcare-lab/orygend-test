Biomedical engineering support

End-to-end biomedical equipment services

From scheduled maintenance and breakdown response to board-level repair and complete outsourced biomedical engineering management, Orygend Healthcare helps hospitals keep critical equipment reliable and ready.

What we do

Services built around your equipment

Explore each service when you need the detail. Every card starts compact so hospitals can quickly compare services without scrolling through long descriptions.

PM

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspection and upkeep that catches faults before they cause downtime.

Routine preventive maintenance planned around your hospital calendar, helping identify wear, faults and performance issues before they become unexpected breakdowns.

AMC

Annual Maintenance Contract

Predictable yearly coverage for servicing, maintenance and priority support.

Contract-based support designed to make equipment upkeep predictable and easier to budget, with scheduled servicing and priority response according to the agreed scope.

CMC

Comprehensive Maintenance Contract

All-inclusive maintenance coverage for facilities seeking complete equipment support.

Comprehensive contract coverage can include labour, agreed spares, scheduled maintenance and breakdown response for critical hospital equipment.

Breakdown

Breakdown Call Support

Rapid-response support when equipment goes down unexpectedly.

We provide breakdown support with initial triage and technical coordination, followed by field service or further escalation as required.

Core expertise

Board-Level Servicing

Fault-tracing and component-level rework on PCBAs and control boards.

Our specialist service focuses on board-level diagnostics and component-level repair, helping recover equipment that may otherwise require expensive board replacement or extended downtime.

Training

Training & Skill Development

Hands-on training for hospital biomedical teams on operation and first-line troubleshooting.

Training can cover equipment operation, first-line troubleshooting, basic upkeep and practical handling relevant to the hospital's biomedical staff.

Managed service

Outsourced Biomedical Engineering Department

Your Biomedical Department, Without the Hassle.

We take complete responsibility for managing your hospital’s biomedical equipment—from preventive maintenance and documentation to vendor coordination and breakdown support—so your hospital team can focus on patient care.

  • Preventive Maintenance (PM)
  • Biomedical equipment documentation
  • Equipment inventory & asset records
  • Service history and maintenance records
  • Breakdown call management
  • Vendor identification & coordination
  • AMC/CMC/service contract coordination
  • Service quotation and technical consultation
  • Equipment inspection and performance checks
  • Internal maintenance coordination
  • New equipment technical consultation
  • Installation and commissioning support
  • Equipment lifecycle management
The key advantage: Orygend Healthcare LLP already has biomedical engineers active in the field and servicing medical equipment across hospitals. This gives your hospital faster access to technical support, vendors and breakdown assistance, without the time and overhead of building a biomedical engineering department from scratch.
