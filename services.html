What we do

End-to-end biomedical equipment support

Seven ways we keep diagnostic and imaging equipment running — from scheduled upkeep and breakdown response to complete outsourced biomedical engineering management.

PM

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspection and upkeep that catches faults before they cause downtime, on a routine calendar you can plan around.

AMC

Annual Maintenance Contract

Yearly coverage for routine servicing and priority response, so equipment upkeep is predictable and budgeted.

CMC

Comprehensive Maintenance Contract

All-inclusive coverage across labour, spares and breakdown response, for facilities that want full peace of mind on critical equipment.

Breakdown

Breakdown Call Support

Rapid-response service calls when equipment goes down unexpectedly, with remote triage before a technician is dispatched.

Core expertise

Board-Level Servicing

Our main specialty: fault-tracing and component-level rework on PCBAs and control boards — recovering equipment other providers write off.

Training

Training & Skill Development

Hands-on training for hospital biomedical staff on operation, first-line troubleshooting and basic upkeep of their equipment.

Managed service

Outsourced Biomedical Engineering Department

Your Biomedical Department, Without the Hassle.

We take complete responsibility for managing your hospital’s biomedical equipment—from preventive maintenance and documentation to vendor coordination and breakdown support—so your hospital team can focus on patient care.

  • Preventive Maintenance (PM)
  • Biomedical equipment documentation
  • Equipment inventory & asset records
  • Service history and maintenance records
  • Breakdown call management
  • Vendor identification & coordination
  • AMC/CMC/service contract coordination
  • Service quotation and technical consultation
  • Equipment inspection and performance checks
  • Internal maintenance coordination
  • New equipment technical consultation
  • Installation and commissioning support
  • Equipment lifecycle management

The key advantage: because our biomedical engineers are already active in the field and servicing medical equipment across hospitals, Orygend Healthcare LLP can provide faster access to technical support, vendors, and breakdown assistance than a hospital starting a biomedical department from scratch.
